Creature - Angulomastacator: Part 5

Other Variants
Lost

Angulomastacator
Angulomastacator are mutated parasaurs! They adapted well to Aberration's radiation, and have gotten larger and stronger because of it! Their unique blood is mildly toxic, so would be attackers tend to avoid them!


How to pronounce
Ann-goo-low-mass-tu-kate-or


Controls & Abilities
Left Click: Head Bash
Right Click: Switch Stance
C/ Thumbstick Right Button: Tail attack - can gather gems - (directional) or roar - scares creatures under 250 dragweight away- (if bipedal)
Space/ Jump: Kick

- Weight reduction for gems

- Stance Switching:
Angulo can switch stances, from Quadrupedal to Bipedal.
Each stance has different move sets
Bipedal is slower, but can turn in place
Quadrupedal moves faster, and drains less stamina, but is less maneuverable

- Special Saddle:
Angulo's saddle protects the rider from radiation and poison (from mushrooms, Basilisk etc)


Spawn Regions
Aberration: Blue Zone
All Other Maps: N/A (unless they have an Aberrant Zone)


How to Tame
Angulomastacator are passive tames. Walk up to them and feed them whatever random food they crave! They have 5 preferred foods! You must be level 60 to tame them.

While normally neutral, they sometimes can get upset by being hand fed by a stranger, and can lash out!

Chibi and Saddle
Wild Angulomastacator have a 10% chance to drop a chibi when killed.
Wild Angulomastacator have a 5% chance to drop a saddle blueprint when killed.

Cryodrakon
The Cryodrakon is a relative of the quetzal. Although it is not strong enough to carry a base on its back, it is much faster than its relative and can even perform diving maneuvers.


How to pronounce
Cry-oh-dray-con


Controls & Abilities
Left Click: Bite / Eat held prey
Right Click: Grab Prey
Jump/Space: Jump/Take off
C/D-pad Down: Wing flap/ blizzard
X/D-pad Up: Ice bomb
Z/Left Shoulder: Icicles (hold to spit, let go to stop)

- Cryodrakon need a running start to take off
- After eating a creature whole, they get a healing buff similar to Argentavis
- Can dive like Griffins and Snow Owls


Ice Meter:
Crydrakon can consume smaller creatures which fills its "Ice Meter" on the left side of your action bar. By filling this up, Cryodrakon can shoot Snowballs (similar to Rock Elementals rock throw) towards enemies which dealing a high amount of dmg. This attack also has a chance to freeze targets.

Ice Feathers:
While Cryo build up the Ice meter, it will cover itself in ice, which will protect it from incoming damage.


Spawn Regions
The Island: Snow
The Center: Snow
Extinction: Snow Dome


How to Tame
Cryodrakon has a 2 Step taming method.

1. Step
You need to damage Cryodrakon. Any method works, but be careful not to over hit them, or it might be hard to tranq them. After loosing 25% of its health, a bright light around its head appears. This is how you know they are ready for phase two.

2. Step
When the light appears, you can tranqshoot Cryo until it falls asleep. They tame with Gem of the Northern King. Which are uncommon drops from killing other Cryo.

Creature - Dracovenator: Part 4


Other Variants
Alpha


Dracovenator
Dracovenator is an avid wyvern hunter. Found along side them and Magmasaurs, this dinosaur is an apex predator. They kill their victims using stolen draconic essence, and are a great addition to any who want to conquer the arks!


How to pronounce
Dray-co-ven-uh-tor


Controls & Abilities
Left-Click: Bite
Right-Click: Mega bite / Elemental breath
C/Right Thumbstick: Claw Swipe
X/Left Shoulder: Tail Slap (directional)
Ctrl/D-pad Down: Cosmetic Roar
R/D-pad Up: Enable/ Disable Essence

Dracovenator can gather 4 different essences from Wyvern, depends on which type of wyvern it is. Use Dracovenator to kill the wyvern to get that essence. Alpha Wyverns give a random mix of essence!

Fire Wyvern - Fire Essence
- Elemental Breath makes fire dmg and burn enemies
- Claw attack burn enemies

Lightning Wyvern - Lightning Essence
- Elemental Breath: makes lightning dmg similar to lightning wyvern
- Claw attack: makes enemies "Zapped" which deals torpor over time

Poison Wyvern - Life Essence
- Elemental Breath: Shoots a gas ball similar to poison wyvern
- Claw Attack: Enemies get the debuff "life cursed" which deals dmg to the target and heal Dracovenator

Ice Wyvern - Ice Essence
- Elemental Breath: makes frost dmg and can freeze the target
- Claw attack: Enemies gets the debuff "Chilled" which slows them down


- Dracovenator deals 2/3 more dmg against wyverns, drakeling, managamrs and magmasaurs and also take 2/3 less dmg from them!
Dracos are also immune to wyverns and Manas breath attacks if you activate the specific essence


Spawn Regions
Scorched Earth: Desert
Extinction: Wastelands
Ragnarok: Desert

How to Tame
To tame Dracovenator, you need an alpha Dracovenator head or fertilized eggs. Magmasaur eggs are the best, followed by Ice wyvern, then Poison/Fire/Lightning, then Crystal wyvern eggs. Other then the special food, it is a traditional KO tame. They have a high amount of torpor, and it drains quickly, so make sure to bring narcotics. If they are using the Poision/Life ability, they cannot be tranqed, so you will have to wait until they switch modes (or you can force them to swap by using an elemental mount such as a Fire wyvern, Magmasaur, Lightning wyvern and so on)



Chibi and Saddle
They have a 10% chance to drop a chibi when killed.
Draco has a 5% chance to drop a saddle bp when killed.

Edmontosaurus has 2 saddles and a helmet. The main saddle allows for one rider. The group saddle has room for a rider and 8 passengers. The helmet can be used with or without a saddle, and increases Edmontosaurus' damage output.

Edmontosaurus doesn't have sharp teeth or claws to deal damage with, so they use their body. When running, they do a lot of trample damage. At max speed, they do even more! Alternatively, you can use a Lightning Wyvern or Jiangjunosaurus in electric mode, albeit, they are less affective.

Once KOd they prefer the highest tier kibble; Extraordinary!

Purussaurus
Purussaurus is a large prehistoric caiman! While not very fast on land, they are very deadly in the water! Their ability to actively manage their fat reserves mean they are very powerful!

Purr-UH-saur-us

The Island: Rivers, Oceans
The Center: Rivers, Oceans


Regurgitate Ability
Tamed Purussaurus (and mounted wild ones while taming) have the ability to convert food into stamina. This is helpful to continue sprinting or attacking, even long after you should have run out of stamina. This ability also plays into their unique armor mechanic.

Food is Power
Depending on your food bar, you can gain different buffs for your Purussaurus. Depending on your playstyle, and how you manage Purussaurus' food, you can enable different positive (or negative) buffs. Below breaks down the food needed, and the buff given:

'Fat Purussaurus': Purussaurus is well fed. This buff is given to Purussaurus with 100-50% food. They move a little bit slower, but take 15% less damage, while dealing 25% more damage

'Skinny Purussaurus': Purussaurus has puked up enough food that they have lost most of their fat reserves. In the water they mode 25% faster, and 15% faster on land. They do not gain armor or melee buffs. This buff is given to Purussaurus who have 49-20% food.

'Malnurished': When Purussaurus' food is below 20% they gain this debuff. They take 25% more damage, and their attacks do 20% less damage. This buff should be avoided!

Note: Only ADULT Purussaurus have these buffs. As babies naturally do not have full food, they cannot gain these buffs. Feeding your Purussaurus will increase their food, changing their hunger state. Regurgitating food is the best way to reduce food, and is a naturally consequence of regurgitation. Managing this is the best way to utilize Purussaurus!


How to Tame
Taming a Purussaurus is very similar to taming a Carcharadontosaurus. They must be tamed in the water, and you will want to make sure you have good armor. Swim up to Purussaurus on a tame, and allow that tame to get grabbed by Purussaurus. The tame must be a drag weight of 500 or less.

After Purussaurus grabs the tame, you need to quick hop on the back of Purussaurus! Once mounted, you need to go around killing creatures. Once enough creatures have been killed, you'll have tamed a Purussaurus! Creatures with a higher drag weight will add more to the taming bar, while smaller ones do less. Either tamed OR wild creatures can be used.

If you don't kill creatures fast enough, you will be bucked off of Purussaurus. This will most likely end in your death. Because Purussaurus are strong, having a good armored creature, or a creature with decent health to be initially grabbed would be helpful. A Turtle might be the best option for a high level Purussaurus.


Chibi and Saddle
10% to drop a chibi when killed
They have a 5% chance to drop a saddle BP when killed

Siamosaurus is a fairly complex creature. In the wild they are neutral with the exception of a few select creatures: Anglerfish, Cnidaria, Coelacanth, Piranha and Sabertooth Salmon. - Immunity to Cnidaria and Electrophorus
- Can gather Angler Gel, Bio Toxin, Black Pearls and Silica Pearls

Flashover
You can use Angler Gel (0 Slot on your hotbar) to make Siamos stronger and faster. They also can do a far stronger tail attack by holding Alt Fire.

As more Angler Gel you feed it, as stronger it becomes. But as more you feed, as less time you have in this modus! You can feed Siamosaurus a max of 40 times before it will reach 100% strength and at this point it will enter berserk mode.

Berserk mode
Siamosaurus will deal 150% damage, gain 5 health per second and its speed will be increased to 125%. At this point you will notice you have a very limited amount of time. You can, however, make good use of the additional 10 times you can feed Siamosaurus a piece of Angler Gel in this stage. When you feed it Angler Gel in berzerk mode, the closer the clock is to 0:00 the more additional bonus time you will earn with the buff.

Once your timer has run out you will enter the sizzled out phase. During this time, Siamosaurus has become worn out and will now need to re-energize itself. Once this debuff is given you will notice your stats have now received a significant penalty. Siamosaurus will now only deal 75% damage, its speed will be decreased to 50% and it will lose 10 stamina per second. The amount of time it takes you to re-energize is completely dependent on how many times you fed your Siamosaurus a piece of Angler Gel. There is quite a large difference in the amount of time it takes to re-energize between feeding it a few Angler Gel and feeding it 50 times.


Spawn Regions
Siamosaurus are commonly found on the coastlines, but due to their aggressiveness towards certain creatures they will sometimes wander out into the water on their own. You will find them naturally spawn in the deeper parts of the Ocean as well. Alpha Siamosaurus are limited to only have a chance to spawn in the ocean waters, not along the beach.
