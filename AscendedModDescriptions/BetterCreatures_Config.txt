All changes in this mod are done via buffs with the only exception of the Defense Unit.

 

Global Config Settings
Below you will find the config settings that affects all tamed creatures.

[BetterCreatures]
DisableAllyLook=True
ReduceFallDamage=True
AllowAllBossTeleporting=True
AllowFlyerBackwardsWalking=False
PreventPassengerDamage=True
PreventSameDinoTargeting=False
PreventLevelUpHealing=True
AllowRiderWeapons=False

AllowMountedDinoBasing=False
DinoBasingWhitelist=DarkPegasus_Character_BP_C,FireLion_Character_BP_C,RockDrake_Character_BP_C
DinoBreedWithVariants=False
WaterDinoForceTurnInPlace=True
DinoBreedWithVariants=True
WaterDinoTakesTorporOnLand=False


DisableAllyLook: Disables creatures from looking at players by default.

ReduceFallDamage: Reduces the amount of damage taken from falling while mounted on a dino.
AllowAllBossTeleporting: Allows creatures with a drag weight of 550 or higher to be teleported (some arena exceptions such as flyers still apply).
AllowFlyerBackwardsWalking: Allows all fliers to walk backwards while landed.
PreventPassengerDamage: Prevents saddles with an active passenger from taking damage.
PreventSameDinoTargeting: Prevents agro from creatures of the same species unless they are attacked. 
PreventLevelUpHealing: Prevents the health regeneration from putting points into health.
AllowRiderWeapons: Allows you to use weapons on all riders (some creatures may have weird issues such as still using attacks).

AllowMountedDinoBasing: Allows players to stand on the back of all tamed creatures (may cause issues in some cases that allows a wild creature to stand on your dino.)
DinoBasingWhitelist: For servers only you can specify the blueprint name separated by a comma that only allows these specific creatures to let players to stand on the back of them. (does not disable vanilla creatures if they are not added). 
WaterDinoForceTurnInPlace: When set to true allows you to turn which direction your'e facing without moving while riding a water tame.
DinoBreedWithVariants: Allows crossbreeding with other creatures such as an aberrant megalosaurus with a vanilla megalosaurus or an S-Spino with a vanilla Spino. (female species is what determines babies).
WaterDinoTakesTorporOnLand When set to true will prevent water dino's from being killed if they manage to exit water but will instead take torpor damage and very quickly become unconscious.

Health Bar HUD
One of the new things added in this mod is the Health Bar HUD which will display a health bar at the bottom of your screen (customizable) that will show the current and max health value on the creature that you're currently riding, this may not work with all creatures such as those that prevent buffs.

 

[BetterCreatures]
PreventHealthHUD=False

 

Levelling Restores Health
A new feature in this mod allows your creatures to restore health each time you level up and put points into it, this will only heal by the amount of extra health that was added.

With more dodo's you will have higher chances that it will affect creatures.

Reaper King / Reaper Queen
All tamed reapers can be configured to not be affected by the light debuff from any sources and have the pregnancy buff changed for more control over how baby reaper level scaling works.

The formular for baby reapers works like this: Wild reaper queen level multiplied by the player level multiplier -1 + bonus xp level.
so lets say you have a level 150 reaper and got all 75 bonus baby reaper levels and the player level multplier achieved is 1.4, this means your baby reaper becomes level 284. (150 wild reaper level multiplied by the player level multiplier of 1.4 = 210 -1 = 209 + 75 = 284.

looking at the ReaperMinPlayerLevel, a value of 50 means that you'll get the minimum multiplier value from ReaperScaleMinLevel so with a player level of 50 or below this will set the player level multiplier to 0.6, the same works for the max level so a player level of 250 multiplies by 1.4 (ReaperScaleMaxLevel) giving you a level 284 baby reaper.

 

Please note for the reaper level scales to work you must set UseNewReaperPregnantBuff=True

[BetterCreatures]
ReaperTamedPreventLightDebuff=False
UseNewReaperPregnantBuff=False
ReaperMinPlayerLevel=50
ReaperMaxPlayerLevel=250
ReaperScaleMinLevel=0.6
ReaperScaleMaxLevel=1.4
PreventReaperQueen=False
PreventReaperKing=False
